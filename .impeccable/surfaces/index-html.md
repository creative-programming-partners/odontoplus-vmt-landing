## Scope

Landing page for OdontoPlus VMT (single route, `/`), full redesign after the user rejected the abstract odontogram concept ("a primera vista no entiendo nada"). Mode: Persuade. Prototype to show the clinic owners.

## Audience and action

Families in Villa María del Triunfo arriving from Instagram/Facebook on phones. They must understand within seconds: dental clinic, in VMT, what it treats, how to book. Action: book through WhatsApp from an appointment card visible in the first viewport. Proof: treatments and cases posted on Instagram, Facebook 100 % recommendation (7 reviews), real address and phone. Stock photos (Unsplash) stand in for clinic photos; demo content stays labelled.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Booking in plain sight. The clinic's paper appointment card (tarjeta de citas) is the hero's booking tool: pick a treatment, a day and a shift, and it becomes the WhatsApp message. Refuses the previous abstract chart concept and any hero that needs explaining.

OWN-WORLD: Clean white and cool mist (#FFFFFF, #F1F6FA) surfaces, OdontoPlus navy (#13264F) for text, logo and full-width bands, one celeste accent (#1E9BD7, from the clinic's Facebook cover) for highlights and icons, WhatsApp green (#1C8F4E) only on WhatsApp actions. Figtree throughout (800 display, 400–600 text). Generous radii (photos 28px, cards 20px), pill buttons, the appointment card as the one elevated object with a navy-tinted shadow, a dashed tear line and a stub. Real photos of dentists, patients and a child at the dentist.

STORY: The visitor reads "dentista en VMT", sees a dentist with a patient, chooses what they need on the card and sends it; scrolling confirms with treatments (with photos), real cases from Instagram, what the first visit is like, opinions, FAQ and the map.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header with new tooth-and-plus logo, nav, phone and "Reservar cita". Left 6 columns: H1 "Tu dentista de confianza en Villa María del Triunfo", one-line promise with the address, and the appointment card as a ticket (treatment chips, day chips, shift on the left; perforated stub on the right with the live summary, "Reservar por WhatsApp" and the phone). No name field: WhatsApp identifies the sender and the button must stay in the first viewport at 1280×720. Right 6 columns: tall rounded photo of a dentist talking with a patient, trust row beneath. Phone: H1, promise, WhatsApp button, photo, then the card.

FORM: "Reserva a la vista" (my grounded candidate 3 of 7, appointment card), chosen by the user from the safer re-roll (round 1, register safer). Seed key 4591a255. Signature interaction: the card's stub fills live as options are chosen ("Endodoncia · jueves por la tarde") and the button sends exactly that. Kept from earlier rounds: live message preview, provenance labels on demo content.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
