## Scope

Landing page for OdontoPlus VMT (single route, `/`). Mode: Persuade. Prototype to show the clinic owners.

## Audience and action

Families in Villa María del Triunfo arriving from Instagram/Facebook on phones, often in pain or anxious. Action: book through WhatsApp (form composes the message live). Proof: real treatment categories and cases posted on Instagram, real address, phone, Facebook 100 % recommendation (7 reviews). Demo content (team, testimonials, hours, two treatments) is labeled "Contenido de ejemplo".

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the clinic's dental chart (odontograma): every treatment is marked on the tooth it solves, red for "por tratar", blue for "tratado". It refuses the category default of a white-and-teal hero with a stock smiling patient and three rounded service cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cool chart paper (#F4F6F3) ruled with hairline grid, navy chart ink (#14254F) carrying whole sections, notation red (#D7263D) and notation blue (#1F5FD1) used only as clinical marks. FDI tooth numbers and field labels in Martian Mono; headings and body in Archivo (expanded width for headings, like a printed form). Components are printed-form parts: ruled rows with fixed columns, underline fields, stamped boxes, the five-surface tooth square. Depth only by flat overlap, no drop shadows.

STORY: The visitor names what they feel, sees it marked on a tooth, learns which treatment fixes it and how many visits it takes, sees real cases from the clinic, understands the first visit, and sends a pre-written WhatsApp message.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Chart-header nav (new logomark: five-surface tooth square with a plus at its centre, wordmark, nav, "Reservar cita"). Left 5 columns: kicker with address, H1 "Tratamos tu sonrisa diente por diente.", one-sentence promise, primary WhatsApp CTA, Facebook recommendation chip. Right 7 columns: the "Ficha" panel at full scale: symptom chips, the full 32-tooth arch (4 quadrant rows on mobile), legend, and the selected tooth's strip with name, treatment and "Consultar por la pieza N".

FORM: Odontograma (my rank 1 of 7; delivered as IMPECCABLE'S PICK over the rolled Cartel Chicha). Seed key 4591a255. Signature interaction: symptom chip -> pen draws a red mark on the tooth -> treatment strip -> WhatsApp prefilled with the tooth number. Raises kept: live WhatsApp message preview (algorave), fixed-column treatment rows (split-flap), provenance labels on every content item (quilt), leader-line annotations on case charts (gyaru), depth only by flat overlap (papercut).
