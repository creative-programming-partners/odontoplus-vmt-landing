# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + Vite + TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Motion for animation. Studio-style structure (app, sections, components, shared, data). Deploy target: Vercel. Confirmed by the user.

## Users

- Families and adults in Villa María del Triunfo (VMT) and southern Lima looking for a nearby dentist they can trust. They usually find the clinic on Instagram or Facebook, on a phone, often while in pain or after putting off treatment.
- Secondary: the clinic owners, who will see this page as a sales prototype prepared by Creative Programming Partners.

## Product Purpose

OdontoPlus VMT is a dental clinic ("consultorio dental para toda la familia"). The site must make a visitor trust the clinic enough to book an appointment through WhatsApp. Success = a visitor reaches WhatsApp with a pre-filled booking message.

## Positioning

A neighbourhood clinic on Av. Pachacútec that shows its real clinical work: restorative and rehabilitation cases (endodontic retreatment, resin replacement, posts, metal-porcelain crowns, full rehabilitation) documented case by case on Instagram. Trust comes from showing the work, not from promises.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Primary action: short form (name, treatment, preferred day/time) that opens WhatsApp with a composed message.
- All copy lives in data files so the prototype can be re-skinned.
- Language: Spanish (Peru).

## Brand Commitments

- Name stays "OdontoPlus VMT". The logo is redesigned (rebrand approved by the user); the navy-blue heritage may be kept or evolved.
- Tagline seen on Facebook cover: "para toda la familia".

## Evidence on Hand

- Real: address, phone, email, social handles, Facebook "recomendado por el 100% (7 opiniones)", the list of treatments posted on Instagram.
- Not on hand (must be marked as example content, never presented as real): doctor names and photos, prices, testimonials text, opening hours, years of experience, patient counts. No invented statistics.

## Product Principles

1. Show the work: real treatment categories, before/after framing, clinical honesty.
2. One clear path to WhatsApp from every section.
3. Calm for anxious patients: explain the process, what to expect, and what it costs to start.
4. Local and close: VMT, Av. Pachacútec, a place you can walk to.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Mobile-first (most traffic from Instagram/Facebook on phones). WCAG AA contrast, large tap targets, reduced-motion support.
